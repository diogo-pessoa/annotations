## The Aspirational Six

1. An intentional life.
2. Master discipline and willpower.
3. My body is my temple.
4. Get "rich", so money is not a concern.
5. Stay curious — gather knowledge, articles, books, journals.
6. Solid foundations — build peace and truth, love and kindness.

## Values

- Be realistically positive.
- Speak with no harsh judgement. — *Marcus Aurelius*
- Be dependable. Share what you have. If asked, spread what works.
- Find ways to help others by sharing what is positive and encouraging.
- Be aware of the bad around you, but stay free of it.
- A fire can't burn long without fuel. — *Marcus Aurelius*
- Leave the sphere of what we are *for others* to them — it lives in their consciousness, not ours; it is the figure we cut in their eyes. — *Arthur Schopenhauer*

### Be true to yourself

- Words match actions.
- Be responsible for your actions; take calculated risks.
- Respect life — never do something that would hurt healthy longevity or long-time relationships.
- You can lie to others, but you can't lie to yourself. You know when you can give more.

### Be practical

- "Success is going from failure to failure without losing your enthusiasm." — *Winston Churchill*
- If not positive, avoid it — be it a relationship, investment, or activity.
  - Positive in this context: if it doesn't add anything, or it takes more than it gives.
  - Example: a friend who is too demanding and has little to offer; a job that has good benefits but does not allow for personal growth.
- Not a priority? Avoid it. If it's not a top-three priority, avoid it at all costs.
- Build a shelter for a rainy day.
- Hell yeah! — or no. Don't do something unless you're certain about it.

### Be kind

- "Use the difficult." — *Michael Caine*
- What can the asshole teach you? Even when it looks bad, there's a good side.
- Practice patience — with those you love, and those you dislike.
- Avoid toxic people; but if you have to deal with them, be short and direct.
- Count and give thanks for your blessings every day.
- Be a better "you" every day.
- Even when it seems there's no action, improve your thinking structure.
- Question (yourself) the way you approach problems and conversations.
- Be assertive as a professional.
- Time is an asset, a non-renewable resource. Use it with people who deserve it, or be alone.
- Make friendship a fine art. Work on your friendships — it's a two-way relationship.
